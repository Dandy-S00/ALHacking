## 2025-05-14 - [Scan Summary]
**Learning:** In security reconnaissance tools, users often deal with a large volume of data across multiple hosts. Providing a high-level "Scan Summary" at the end of the execution serves as a delightful micro-UX improvement by giving the user an immediate sense of the scan's impact and scale without needing to scroll through or aggregate the details manually.
**Action:** Always include a high-level summary block at the end of long-running or multi-stage CLI processes to improve results visibility and user satisfaction.
